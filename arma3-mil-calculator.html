Model: point-mass trajectory, RK2 integration, standard G1/G7 drag curves scaled by each round's ballistic coefficient and true bore diameter/mass. Air density and speed of sound follow the input temperature at fixed sea-level pressure (1013.25 hPa, dry air). Muzzle velocity shifts with temperature using the same per-round curves as ACE3's Advanced Ballistics, since vanilla Arma 3 ammo doesn't model temperature at all. The bore starts below the sight line by the entered height-over-bore and elevation is solved so the trajectory crosses that line at the zero range, which also reproduces the short "false zero" close to the muzzle. Spin drift uses the Miller stability formula (from twist rate, bullet length/diameter/weight, velocity, and air temperature) fed into Litz's simplified drift formula (1.25·(Sg+1.2)·ToF^1.83), assuming a right-hand twist barrel — flip the sign if yours is left-hand. Bullet length isn't in the source data you gave me, so it's pre-filled with an estimate for 6.5mm ELD-M bullets; edit it if you know the real figure; drift is fairly insensitive to small errors here. Wind and Coriolis are still not modeled.
